READ: the refusals are NOT junk. Charging a deliberately generous round-trip impact,
every refused cohort stays strongly net-positive, and SIZING DOWN to 0.25 SOL (4x
less impact) improves every cohort. Two proposals follow from this and BOTH change
what the system can do, so they are operator decisions, not housekeeping:
  D1 size-to-fit: when the ruled clip is vetoed, take the smaller clip instead of
     refusing (touches the venue-conditional action space / label meaning).
  D2 curve fallback: when the AMM is stale but the CURVE has capacity and impact
     <= veto, route the fill to the curve at 0.25 (live venue resolution).
NOT proposed: raising the 90 bp threshold itself — sizing down preserves the cost
discipline that the threshold encodes.
