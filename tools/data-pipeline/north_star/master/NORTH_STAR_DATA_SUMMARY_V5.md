## Data we need
1. **Source truth:** rights/license, raw hashes, collection versions, timestamps, outages and completeness.
2. **Identity/mechanics:** chain/mint, creator/authority evidence, token extensions, curve/pool state and migration.
3. **Trades/execution:** signed balances, real fills, transfers, fees/tips, failed orders, submission/confirmation times.
4. **Capacity:** executable size-dependent quotes, reserves, exit routes, congestion and latency.
5. **Charts/flow:** trailing price structure, momentum/exhaustion, buying/selling persistence, liquidity divergence.
6. **Wallet/holder risk:** qualified holder data, creator/connected-wallet activity, prior skill including open losers.
7. **Narrative meaning:** catalysts, cultural context, originality, rumor/fact/satire, OG/derivative relationships and counterevidence.
8. **Propagation/crowding:** independent attention, repost chains, source diversity, promotion and saturation; attention versus flow timing.
9. **Meta/rotations:** emerging/weakening/reviving themes, competing tokens, capital rotation and correlated exposure.
10. **Portfolio/opportunities:** eligible alternatives, cash/pending orders, inventory, existing thesis, invalidation and expiry.
11. **Decisions:** real human or clearly typed action-only episodes covering entry, wait, hold, trim, runner, exit and re-entry.
12. **Outcomes:** realized net PnL, remaining executable equity, failures, censoring, per-token/wallet/theme distributions.
13. **Mechanics/numeracy:** source-backed protocol knowledge and tested exact accounting.
14. **Rust quality:** separate real-code/review/test-evidence track, not bulk filler in the trader corpus.

## Main concepts
- Whole sequential portfolios, not isolated winning buys.
- Narrative + numerical evidence at the same decision cutoff; no hindsight context.
- Do not equate narrative strength with a good entry, or a price collapse with a proven rug.
- Original source spans and actual human judgments; no AI-written training rationales.
- Actual fills, modeled outcomes and expert decisions remain distinct.
- Permissive source licenses plus reporting/approval before inclusion; public does not mean licensed.
- Freeze evaluation boundaries BEFORE fitting features, teacher rankings or execution models.
- Calibrate economic replay against actual executions; limits must survive uncertainty and cost/latency stress.
- No padding/duplicating to reach tokens. Task mixture uses useful post-mask supervision, not volume.
- Low training loss is not profitability; require baselines, shadow evidence and separately approved live proof.
