# Evaluation reservation decision — operator-approved principle

## Operator direction

Following the proposal for future protected economic sessions and a separate balanced diagnostic suite, the operator said: “If your recommendation is the best option for the best quality dataset then fine, if it adds unnecessary complexity then don’t do it”.

Interpretation: proceed with the minimal master-required evaluation separation; do not build a separate collection platform or add speculative infrastructure. This is approval in principle, not approval of unspecified numeric risk limits, spending overages, training or live orders.

## Selected approach

Use the existing capture pipeline and a small versioned source/time/split manifest. Preregister future windows and objective coverage/outage rules before collection. Keep those sessions inaccessible to training, tuning, teacher selection and retrieval construction. Do not inspect their outcomes to select or replace windows. Keep natural-prevalence economic sessions separate from the balanced action diagnostic suite.

No extra parallel stream is required just to create an evaluation split. Use the same permitted collection budget; routing selected sessions to protected custody is a dataset-assignment change, not a new service. Missing narrative, clocks or lineage still block full evaluation support.

This protects measurement credibility and prevents contamination; it does not itself increase label quality or guarantee model performance. A train-only corpus without an untouched evaluation cannot support the master’s final economic claims.

## Exact reservation remains pending

The current EVAL_FREEZE policy remains pending until exact future boundaries, custody enforcement, coverage criteria and required dependency windows are recorded and tested. Do not mark Stage 1 complete based on this principle approval. Existing inspected historical and Megga sessions stay DEVELOPMENT/EXPOSED.
